Core process

What we run every shift

AF-01

Hot & cold forging

Closed-die presses from 400T to 2500T with in-house die design and try-out presses for faster SOP. Induction billet heaters and controlled cool-down for grain structure.

  • Crankshafts, knuckles, hubs
  • Connecting rods & yokes
  • Alloy steels & microalloy grades
AF-02

CNC / VMC machining

Horizontal and vertical machining centres with pallet pools for lights-out shifts on stable SKUs. Tool-life dashboards tied to OEE cells.

  • 5-axis complex housings
  • Drivetrain flanges & carriers
  • EV axle & bracket families
AF-03

Heat treatment

Batch and continuous furnaces with atmosphere control — carburising, carbonitriding, induction harden, and temper with charted recipes per heat.

  • Case depth verification
  • Induction patterns for shafts
  • Temper charts per heat

NABL-aligned practices with CMM, contour, surface, and metallography supporting PPAP Level 3 and customer portal uploads.

  • Serialised pack-out

Materials & envelope

Technical range

Materials Carbon & alloy steels (EN8, EN19, 20MnCr5, 16MnCr5, 42CrMo4), microalloy grades, selective stainless for industrial housings
Forging mass 0.4 kg to ~45 kg finished weight depending on geometry and press line
Machining tolerance Typical ±0.02 mm on critical bores; tighter on CMM-controlled features by agreement
Surface & finish As-forged, shot blast, phosphated, oil dipped; Ra targets per drawing with contour verification
Lot traceability Heat number → forge lot → machine cell → dispatch — barcode / QR on pack labels
Monthly capacity ~1.8 lakh finished pieces across forge + machine (SKU mix dependent)
